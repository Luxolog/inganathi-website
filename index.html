01 / WHO WE ARE

Building trust.

Inganathi Driving Academy is a 100% black-owned, managed and resourced company.

Since its inception, the academy has invested extensively in research and development to create driver-training packages that are relevant to the communities it serves. Its intention is to help develop a pool of well-skilled drivers, recognising that a driver’s licence is an essential employment requirement in South Africa.

We understand that nobody can claim to have remained untouched by road carnage in our country, whether directly or indirectly. This is why we believe that putting greater effort into road-safety awareness has never been more urgent.

The driver-training market is vast, and there are no real secrets in the industry. We may be able to fool someone for a day, but never forever. That is why we work hard to provide a top-level service for two reasons:

  1. To be worthy of your trust.
  2. So that when someone you know—a family member or friend—needs driver-training services in the future, you can confidently recommend Inganathi Driving Academy as a reliable choice.

We are working to dispel the myth that “quality is linked to price”.

06 / LEADERSHIP & LEGACY

Uviwe Y. Gozongo

Founder · Inganathi Driving Academy

Uviwe Y. Gozongo founded Inganathi Driving Academy with a commitment to professional driver training and safer roads.

On 25 January 2015, he was elected Secretary General of the Eastern Cape Driving Schools Council when it was established by the then MEC of Transport, Ms Weziwe Tikana-Gxothiwe, as part of a pathway towards regulation of the industry in the province.

Following the dissolution of that council, the Eastern Cape Driving Schools Interim Task Team was launched on 5 March 2026 by the MEC of Transport, Mr Xolile Nqatha. Mr Gozongo was elected Secretary of the interim task team.

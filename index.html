01 / WHO WE ARE

Empowering drivers.
Building safer communities.

Inganathi Driving Academy was established in 2009 and has since helped hundreds of people in Mthatha and surrounding areas gain confidence, prepare for and pass their learner’s licence and driving tests, and develop the skills to become safe, responsible drivers through structured K53 training.

We believe quality driver education is about more than passing a test. It is about equipping people with pertinent knowledge, sound judgement and practical skills to make our roads safer for everyone.

05 / LEADERSHIP & LEGACY

Uviwe Y. Gozongo

Founder · Inganathi Driving Academy

Uviwe Y. Gozongo founded Inganathi Driving Academy with a commitment to professional driver training and safer roads.

On 25 January 2015, he was elected Secretary General of the Eastern Cape Driving Schools Council when it was established by the then MEC of Transport, Ms Weziwe Tikana-Gxothiwe, as part of a pathway towards regulation of the industry in the province.

Following the dissolution of that council, the Eastern Cape Driving Schools Interim Task Team was launched on 5 March 2026 by the MEC of Transport, Mr Xolile Nqatha. Mr Gozongo was elected Secretary of the interim task team.
